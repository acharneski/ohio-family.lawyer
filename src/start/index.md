## First steps for anyone

1. **Read every document you were served, today.** Note every deadline and hearing date. Missing one can decide the case.
2. **Find legal help.** Start with [legal aid and low-cost options](/resources/#directory). Many courts also have self-help centers.
3. **Do not post about your case.** Social media, texts, and emails can be used as evidence. Assume the judge will read them.
4. **Start a clean record.** Keep a dated, factual log of parenting time, communication, and payments. See the guide *Documentation without drama* (coming soon).
5. **Learn the vocabulary.** "Residential parent," "allocation of parental rights," "GAL." See the [glossary](/resources/glossary/).
