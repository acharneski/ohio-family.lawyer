Grandparent and relative rights in Ohio depend heavily on the circumstances: whether the parents were married, whether a parent has died, and whether a case is already open. Confirm with an attorney.
{% endcallout %}

## The short version

Ohio allows grandparents and other relatives to ask a court for companionship or visitation in certain situations. Examples include during a divorce or custody case, after a parent's death, or when the child's parents were unmarried. The court decides based on the child's best interest, and parents' wishes carry significant weight.

## First steps

1. Find out whether a case involving the child is already open, and in which court.
2. Gather a record of your existing relationship with the child.
3. Contact [legal aid](/resources/#directory). Eligibility rules vary.
