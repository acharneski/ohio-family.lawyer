## Right now

- **Emergency:** call [911](tel:911).
- **Mental-health or suicide crisis:** call or text [988](tel:988).
- **Domestic violence:** National Domestic Violence Hotline, [1-800-799-7233](tel:18007997233), or text START to 88788.

## Protective orders in Ohio

A **civil protection order** (ORC § 3113.31) is available for domestic violence. An emergency (ex parte) order can be issued quickly, followed by a full hearing. Courts and local DV programs can help you file.

**If a protective order has been filed against you:** follow it exactly, even if you believe it is false, and get legal help before the full hearing. Violating an order is a separate offense.
