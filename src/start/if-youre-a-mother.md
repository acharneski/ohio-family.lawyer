## Where you start

If you were not married when your child was born, Ohio law makes you the sole residential parent and legal custodian until a court orders otherwise (ORC § 3109.042). That default can change quickly once a case is filed, so it helps to understand the process early.

## Practical first steps

1. **Get support established through your county's child support enforcement agency (CSEA)**, or through the court.
2. **If there is any safety concern**, document it and see [Safety first](/start/safety-first/). Protective orders exist for genuine danger and should be fast to obtain.
3. **Think about a workable schedule.** Courts weigh each parent's willingness to support the child's relationship with the other parent.
4. **Keep a factual log**, not a diary of grievances.
