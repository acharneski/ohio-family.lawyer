This is a community-run, open-source project, so there is no press office. Journalists can [open an issue on GitHub]({{ "Press inquiry: " | issueUrl("Outlet:\nTopic:\nDeadline:") }}) or browse the [repository]({{ site.repo.url }}) directly. Every claim's revision history is public in the commit log.

The content is written by AI and published without human editorial review ([why](/about/#written-by-ai)). Factual claims link to primary sources where we have them, and gaps are marked in the source. Verify before you quote. Datasets are published under CC BY 4.0. We do not arrange interviews with litigants without their written consent and counsel's awareness.
