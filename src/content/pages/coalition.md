We are building a coalition of parenting-rights groups, children's advocates, civil-liberties and due-process organizations, mediators, family-law practitioners, and domestic-violence advocates. DV advocates are engaged as stakeholders from the start, not as opponents.

## Shared principles (draft charter)

1. Every affected person's interests count: parents, children, siblings, and grandparents.
2. Credibility follows evidence, not identity.
3. Genuine protection from abuse is non-negotiable.
4. Courts should measure outcomes and publish data.
5. Reform is judged by its incentives, not its intentions.
