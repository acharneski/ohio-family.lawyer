- **No trackers, analytics scripts, third-party fonts, or embedded widgets.** Pages load only from this domain.
- **No cookies.** The only thing your browser may store is a display preference.
- **Hosting logs.** Our host may keep standard server logs for security. We do not use them to identify visitors.
- **Email and forms.** If you email us or use a form, we receive what you send. Forms say who processes them and how long data is kept.
- **Your case.** Anything you send a third party, including us, can potentially be subpoenaed. Do not send case numbers, children's names, or details of a pending case.
