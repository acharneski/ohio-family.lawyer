- **No trackers, analytics scripts, third-party fonts, or embedded widgets.** Pages load only from this domain.
- **No cookies.** The only thing your browser may store is a display preference.
- **Hosting logs.** Our host may keep standard server logs for security. We do not use them to identify visitors.
- **GitHub.** This is an open-source project with no mailbox and no contact forms. Issues, pull requests, and comments on our [GitHub repository]({{ site.repo.url }}) are public and are handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy. Links to GitHub are ordinary links. Nothing from GitHub loads on our pages.
- **Your case.** Anything you post publicly or send to a third party can potentially be used in litigation. Never post case numbers, children's names, or details of a pending case.
