## Crisis lines

- **Emergency:** [911](tel:911)
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline:** call or text [988](tel:988)
- **National Domestic Violence Hotline:** [1-800-799-7233](tel:18007997233), or text START to 88788

## Peer support

A vetted list of father-inclusive and mother-inclusive peer groups across Ohio is in progress.

{% callout "note", "This is not therapy" %}
This site offers information, not treatment. Litigation is one of the most stressful experiences a person can go through. Getting support is a strength, not an admission of anything. Talk to your attorney about confidentiality.
