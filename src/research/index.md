All datasets will be published as CSV and JSON with a methodology note under CC BY 4.0.

## Projects

1. **Ohio Family Court Transparency Index.** County-by-county case volume, time to disposition, shared versus sole orders, GAL appointment rates, and contempt filings, built from Supreme Court of Ohio statistical reports and public-records requests (ORC § 149.43). *Status: planning.*
2. **Public records request toolkit.** Templates and a public log of requests and responses, so findings are reproducible. *Status: planning.*
3. **Cost-of-conflict estimates.** The typical cost of a contested case, with every assumption published. *Status: planning.*
4. **Outcomes gap analysis.** What Ohio does not measure, and what research elsewhere has found. *Status: planning.*
