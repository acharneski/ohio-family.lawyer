## 1. Find your legislators

Use the official lookup at the [Ohio General Assembly](https://www.legislature.ohio.gov/) (opens the official site; we embed no lookup or geolocation).

## 2. Contact them

Call and email scripts tailored to each active bill will appear on the [bill tracker](/reform/bill-tracker/). The general shape is:

> I'm a constituent in [city]. I'm calling about [bill number]. I [support/oppose] it because [one sentence]. Please let me know where [legislator] stands.

## 3. Testify

Ohio committees accept **proponent**, **opponent**, and **interested-party** testimony. Templates with 3-minute and 5-minute versions are in progress. Until then:

- Lead with who you are and the one fact that matters most.
- Speak about the system, not your ex. Never name private individuals or children.
- End with a specific request.

## 4. Share your story (with consent)

A consent-first story intake form is coming. You will be able to choose **never publish**, **anonymized only**, or **named**. We will never ask for case numbers or children's names. Statements made to third parties can be subpoenaed, so talk to your attorney first.

## 5. Public comment calendar

Supreme Court of Ohio rule-comment periods, ODJFS rulemaking, and JCARR dates will be listed here.
